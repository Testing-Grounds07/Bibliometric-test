What is a citation combination?

Articles that appear together in other papers’ reference lists. A hypergraph keeps that whole combination—not just a separate line for each pair.

Why might the methods disagree?

Leiden checks connections and group size. Infomap checks how compactly movement can be described. Direct hypergraph checks how whole combinations stay together beyond its expected baseline. Different rules can agree—or disagree. Neither outcome proves a research theme.

How this matches BiblioMap

In co-citation, the circles are cited references and combinations are supported jointly by source papers. In coupling, the circles are source papers and a group can represent papers sharing a reference. Both views still need researcher review. Reliability checks assess sensitivity—not whether a theme is true.
