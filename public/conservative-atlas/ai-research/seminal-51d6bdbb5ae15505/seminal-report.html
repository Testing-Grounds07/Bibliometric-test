Where to start with seminal articles in adaptive leadership

Start with Yukl & Mahsud (2010) and DeRue (2011). They combine substantial observed uptake with broad conceptual contributions inside the existing selection. Then choose a branch: the earlier Glover formulation, Heifetz applications, clinical frameworks, or adaptive team leadership.

These are provisional candidates and reading priorities. The evidence supports a useful reading route; it does not establish a definitive historical ranking. Several foundational Heifetz works are books or have titles outside this corpus.
