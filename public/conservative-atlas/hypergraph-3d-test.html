What am I seeing?

This test uses the adaptive leadership study, reference scope ≥10, combined orders 2–10. Leiden or Infomap clusters the whole-group incidence network. It does not change your main map or saved projects.

Each floor shows the cluster's articles. Each raised plane shows one exact supported group, including outside-cluster members where applicable. Dotted guides repeat article identity, not citation links.

All clusters are included, but only the selected number of groups per cluster are displayed, prioritizing 3+ member combinations, then stronger support. Pairs fill remaining slots. Cross-cluster groups can repeat in each participating cluster's stack, always with their full membership. Repeated layers are the same evidence; do not add their support counts. Stacks with no groups still show their floor. Layout and height do not measure similarity or importance.
