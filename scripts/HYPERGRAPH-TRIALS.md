# Hypergraph method trials

Open `conservative-atlas/hypergraph-lab.html`, linked under More → Resources in the main workspace. This standalone page uses the published, deduplicated adaptive-leadership snapshot (2,855 sources), reference scopes 10/20/30, minimum support five and exact co-citation combinations. Combined orders are capped at 10. It does not retrieve new literature, change projects, upload graphs, apply methods to private evidence or replace the main map. Results live in the page session; export JSON to retain settings, memberships, diagnostics, source identifiers/version and optional audit.

All five trial choices use the same available weighted combinations, except categorical mode explicitly excludes combinations with no usable supporting-source year. `buildHyperedges` reconstructs combinations and normalizes each participating source's total unit across the included combinations. Limits: 800 linked articles, 20,000 whole groups, 200,000 incidences; excess input fails, never truncates. UI work runs in a terminable dedicated worker. The v2 trial engine adds pinned MIT-licensed `ml-matrix` 6.15.0 for symmetric eigendecomposition, bundled locally; dependency notices are supplied alongside the lab. No services, database or authentication configuration are added.

## Implementations and boundaries

- **Spectral** uses `I - Dv^-1/2 H W De^-1 H^T Dv^-1/2`. V2 constructs the symmetric normalized operator for each connected component and uses `ml-matrix` symmetric EVD, replacing the slowly converging fixed 600-step subspace iteration. Every selected eigenvector is checked against the original sparse operator: absolute residual ≤1e-8 and pairwise orthogonality error ≤1e-8. These are stricter numerical checks, not relaxed thresholds. Eigenvalue boundary gaps are reported; tied values imply non-unique embeddings, not necessarily solver failure. Row normalization and three seeded k-means starts (60 iterations) follow; k-means can still report its own limit. The reported objective remains the Zhou normalized cut, not ordinary cut-net. Connected components are solved independently and never combined; effective k increases when component count exceeds requested k. Additional groups are allocated by component size/current group count. This allocation is a heuristic, not globally optimal. Residuals and limit flags are retained; no tensor decomposition is claimed. AON's spectral initialization also changes, so its deterministic fitted partition can differ from v1.
- **Degree-adjusted AON approximation** profiles separate assortative within/outside rates for each hyperedge size using fixed propensities `theta_i = weighted_degree_i / total_volume`. The within exposure is `sum_g volume_fraction_g^d`; pooled inside/outside masses yield the log-likelihood ratio against a common-rate null, constrained to assortativity. Fractional weights mean this is a weighted quasi-likelihood objective. Three seeded label searches (one spectral-initialized) keep nonempty groups, with at most 15 passes. This is NOT joint estimation of theta and affinities, NOT a full DCHSBM implementation, not Bayesian inference and not an MDL-selected k. The first spectral initialization may retain more groups than requested to preserve disconnected components. Quality is not evidence probability or a cross-family comparison.
- **Balanced cut** minimizes the sum of weights of whole groups cut by the partition. A maximum block size `ceil((1 + epsilon) * n / k)` is enforced, with no empty blocks. Three shuffled balanced initializations now get up to 96 rounds, with incremental exact cut gains instead of repeatedly modifying/recomputing partitions for rejected proposals. Each round checks feasible single-article moves and samples up to 12 swaps per article. Before declaring a result settled, the engine exhaustively checks ALL feasible single-article moves and ALL two-article swaps at the current partition, using improvement tolerance 1e-10. An improving verification proposal triggers further refinement; if the round budget is exhausted, the result remains provisional and reports a remaining improvement. This verifies a local neighborhood only, not global optimality or multilevel FM/hMETIS/KaHyPar. Equal sizes are a requested constraint, not evidence for natural communities. No hardcoded vertex-count routing rules are used.

**Continue refinement** appears for provisional balanced results. It retains their pinned evidence, memberships and bounds, runs one warm-started refinement with double the previous round budget (at most 512), and records checkpoint lineage. It does not restart from a new random partition, drop nodes, relax balance, or erase the previous trial. Resampling deliberately starts afresh rather than reusing original-evidence checkpoints. `hypergraph-trials-v2` identifies the changed implementation, and checking a v1 result with the v2 worker asks for a rerun rather than silently mixing versions.
- **Local** uses personalized PageRank on the weighted article–group incidence walk, including self returns (restart 0.15 by default, at most 1,000 iterations, L1 residual 1e-9). Rank by probability/degree and sweep seed-containing prefixes of at most half the total volume, selecting the smallest whole-hyperedge cut-weight/min-side-volume. If none qualifies, retain the seed singleton. Only the returned neighborhood is shown; outside articles are not called unlinked. This is a custom diffusion/sweep baseline, not HyperLocal's flow algorithm. A missing seed during resampling returns no members rather than choosing a different seed.
- **Categorical demo** derives an explicit edge category from its supporting articles' modal publication decade, ties resolved toward the earlier decade. Counts and rule are traceable; missing-year groups are excluded and coverage reported. Three seeded categorical vertex-label searches (weighted-vote initialization plus two random starts) maximize the weight of edges whose members all receive the edge's category, with at most 20 passes. Node labels are decade categories, not thematic communities. This is a heuristic for the categorical agreement objective, not the paper's approximation algorithm and not tensor clustering. A real semantic categorical application requires independent justified edge labels.

The trials now use the main canvas shell: the real cluster strip, cluster visibility/connected-cluster filters, article search, pan/zoom, author/keyword/Qwen heading modes, automatic evidence-backed AI naming, article/line inspection, envelopes, exploded combinations and 3D. Trial parameters stay in the floating Analysis panel; the compact top chooser selects the experimental method. The previous completed map remains visible while another fit runs. Session history and JSON export retain the trial parameters and diagnostics. Original pairwise lines remain navigation context—not the scoring objective. No score is compared across families to declare a winner.

`build-trial-workspace.mjs` derives the lab HTML and app/shell assets from the same sources as the main workspace. Trial-only bundles keep these experimental adapters isolated from the published main map. The lab stays pinned to the public adaptive-leadership snapshot; project writes, coupling, temporal reclustering, subcluster recomputation and automatic trial settings selection are not exposed. Missing or excluded memberships are unassigned, never inherited from the baseline partition. The main production bundles need not change.

## Reliability

The default trial canvas shows hyperedge envelopes, not just the pair projection. The shared Canvas chooser is visible in the top toolbar and offers envelopes, pair connections and 3D. Envelopes show up to 60 strongest complete eligible combinations with at least three members; pair lines remain citation context. Cluster filters narrow the eligible envelopes, and the original exploded/3D views expose exact groups. Switching trial methods retains an explicit display choice; this never changes clustering or analytical evidence.

V2 verification on the pinned ≥10-reference, combined 2–10 adaptive-leadership evidence (396 linked references, 8,012 combinations), at requested k=24: both spectral and balanced fits complete their respective numerical/local-neighborhood checks. The selected balanced start settles after 39 rounds, rather than stopping at 12; its final whole-cut cost is 415.8650851184976. The largest spectral eigenvector residual is approximately 1.82e-14. Ten conditional resamples of each method have zero solver-limit hits. A real-evidence one-round balanced checkpoint was also continued to a verified local result, retaining evidence and bounds and decreasing cut cost. These are computation checks, not evidence of a best partition or thematic validity. Machine-specific timings and raw diagnostics are in `.trials/trial-limits-verification.json`; focused regression results are in `.trials/trial-limit-tests.txt`.

Check evidence runs ten source bootstraps with replacement, rebuilds support and source-normalized weights on the fixed discovered combinations, and reruns the SAME chosen family and settings. One search start per resample makes this a preliminary sensitivity diagnostic including optimization variation; it is not identical compute effort to the three-start original. Local results keep the selected seed. Period categories remain fixed, so their derivation is not revalidated. Report best-Jaccard recovery/retention using the existing evidence-guidance rules and count limit-hit samples. No Auto CPM or automatic settings recommendation is offered; k, balance and restart are explicit experimental choices.
